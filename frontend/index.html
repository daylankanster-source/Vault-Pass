What VaultPass adds to physical ownership

I.

Provable ownership

Every serial number lives on-chain, linked to one wallet. A skeptical buyer can verify authenticity in seconds.

II.

Transferable identity

Selling your camera? Transfer its VaultPass to the buyer in one transaction. The pass follows the object — every previous owner is on-record.

III.

On-chain warranty

File a warranty claim directly against the pass. Approved resolvers (manufacturers, authorities) respond on-chain — no lost paperwork, no email limbo.

IV.

Deactivation & recovery

Lost or destroyed? Mark the pass inactive. A stolen device with a deactivated pass is worthless on the resale market.
